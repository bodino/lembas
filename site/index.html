How it works

Mac to Mac, with nothing in between

When you hit your limit, your request goes to a friend's Mac that's away. It adds its login to one encrypted record and passes it on. It never sees your prompt, you never see its login, and it can't change the answer you get back.

Your Mac

Runs the TLS session to Anthropic and keeps its keys.

TLS 1.2 · AES 128 GCM

Your friend's Mac

Splices its token into one record. Relays everything else, unable to read or alter it.

zero knowledge keystream proof

Anthropic

Gets a normal Claude Code request on your friend's subscription.

api.anthropic.com

Your friend's Mac can't change a single byte of the answer. Every record is sealed with a key only your Mac and Anthropic hold.

Friends lend only while they're away, up to 70% of their five hour window. If nobody has room, you wait for your own reset, as always.
